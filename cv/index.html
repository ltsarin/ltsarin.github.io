Professional Summary

Computational biologist working at the intersection of cancer genomics, genome instability, and molecular biology. I study how errors made by the replicative DNA polymerases shape tumour genomes and how we can detect it — combining machine learning on tumour sequencing data, curation of the primary functional literature, and yeast genetics to connect a variant to the phenotype it causes. First author of PolED, a curated database of functional studies of POLE and POLD1 variants in humans.
