I'm a Research Scholar in the Shcherbakova Lab at the University of Nebraska Medical Center,
where I work at the intersection of cancer genomics, genome instability, and computational biology. My current research focuses on *POLE*/*POLD1* tumors, specifically their unique mutational signatures that extend beyond the classic COSMIC catalog. I aim to enhance our understanding of these signatures through machine learning algorithms, on top of developing clinically relevant tools to stratify cancer patients who might benefit from personalized treatment approaches.
Recently, we published [PolED](https://doi.org/10.1093/database/baaf076), a database that helps researchers and clinicians interpret *POLE*/*POLD1* variants using aggregated functional evidence.

Outside the dry-lab, I commute year-round on an electric unicycle — one-legged, if the mood strikes. Being a tech & science nerd, I have to remain adventurous, falling in love with nature, hiking and explore new places.
